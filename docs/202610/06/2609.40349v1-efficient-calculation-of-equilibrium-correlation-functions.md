## 摘要
物理系统中大多数可测量的动力学量都可以描述为形式为 $G_{AB}(t-t') = \left<A(t)B(t')\right>$ 的平衡关联函数，其中 $t'$ 表示作用时刻，$t$ 表示响应时刻。传统的用于计算此类量的量子算法，例如基于哈密顿量模拟的 Hadamard 测试、变分方法或基于线性响应的算法，都有一个共同特征：每个时间点 $t-t'$ 都需要由不同的量子电路计算，导致总采样次数至少为 $O(N_t)$，总量子门数至少为 $O(N_t^2)$。在这里，我们提供一种不同的方法：利用 $O(\log N_t)$ 个辅助量子比特将时间存储为量子变量，并通过对所有时间点仅需一个量子电路，有效并行化平衡关联函数的计算。我们表明，该电路只需运行 $O(\log N_t)$ 次，在大系统尺寸极限下总共需要 $O(N_t \log N_t)$ 的量子资源。我们证明这种标度是最优的，并演示了我们的算法计算一维无自旋 Hubbard 模型的格林函数。

## Abstract
A majority of measurable dynamic quantities of a physical system is described as an equilibrium correlation function of the form $G_{AB}(t-t') = \left<A(t)B(t')\right>$, where $t'$ represents the impact time and $t$ represents the response time. Conventional quantum algorithms to compute such quantities via Hamiltonian simulation such as the Hadamard test, variational methods, or linear-response-based algorithms share one feature in common: each time point $t-t'$ is calculated by a different quantum circuit, leading to at least $O(N_t)$ total shots, and at least $O(N_t^2)$ total quantum gates. Here, we provide a different approach: utilizing $O(\log N_t)$ ancilla qubits to store time as a quantum variable, and effectively parallelize the computation of an equilibrium correlation function by requiring only a single quantum circuit for all time points. We show that this circuit needs to be run only $O(\log N_t)$ times, which in total requires $O(N_t \log N_t)$ quantum resources in the large system size limits. We prove that this scaling is optimal, and demonstrate our algorithm calculating the Green's function of a one-dimensional spinless Hubbard model.
