## 摘要
瞬态热传导的隐式离散会反复生成稀疏线性系统，使扩散问题成为量子线性求解器的天然试验平台。本研究评估变分量子线性求解器（VQLS）在 IBM 量子硬件上对一维传导进行一次后向欧拉步所得温度场的恢复精度。一项预先设定的验证性实验比较 N=4 个内部温度（编码于两个量子比特）与 N=8 个温度（编码于三个量子比特），使用十个配对硬件区块；另行定义的 N=16、四量子比特扩展在五个独立调度的探索性区块中评估。局部 VQLS 优化将相对温度场误差降至约 10^{-8}（所有三种情形），在此尺度上建立了本质上精确的理想参考。在硬件上，N=4 的平均相对 L2 温度场误差为 8.07%，N=8 为 20.14%，配对增加 12.06 个百分点（95% CI，10.71–13.42；精确配对符号翻转 p=0.001953）。探索性 N=16 的均值为 24.75±1.84%。随后一项预先设定的 N=16 散粒数随访在六个独立调度的区块中，以 2,048、8,192 和 32,768 次采样评估同一锁定电路。平均场误差分别保持为 23.64%、24.57% 和 24.37%；32,768 减 2,048 次采样的对比为 +0.74 个百分点（95% CI，-0.65 至 2.12；p=0.281）。相比之下，理想多项采样预测从 2.68% 降至 0.75%。更高的采样次数降低了区块间变异性，但未能恢复平均冷侧温度场。结果表明，在测试的硬件条件下，强非均匀热状态的全场读出受到的限制超出普通有限采样，这促使针对热流、储存能量和选定温度构建以可观测量为重点的量子表述。

## Abstract
Implicit discretizations of transient heat conduction repeatedly generate sparse linear systems, making diffusion problems a natural test bed for quantum linear solvers. This study evaluates how accurately a variational quantum linear solver (VQLS) recovers the temperature field produced by one backward-Euler step of one-dimensional conduction on IBM quantum hardware. A pre-specified confirmatory experiment compares N=4 interior temperatures encoded on two qubits with N=8 temperatures encoded on three qubits using ten paired hardware blocks; a separately defined N=16, four-qubit extension is evaluated in five independently scheduled exploratory blocks. Local VQLS optimization reduces relative temperature-field error to approximately 10^{-8} for all three cases, establishing an essentially exact ideal reference at this scale. On hardware, mean relative L2 temperature-field error is 8.07% for N=4 and 20.14% for N=8, a paired increase of 12.06 percentage points (95% CI, 10.71-13.42; exact paired sign-flip p=0.001953). The exploratory N=16 mean is 24.75+/-1.84%. A pre-specified N=16 shot-count follow-up then evaluates the same locked circuit at 2,048, 8,192, and 32,768 shots in six independently scheduled blocks. Mean field error remains 23.64%, 24.57%, and 24.37%, respectively; the 32,768-minus-2,048-shot contrast is +0.74 percentage points (95% CI, -0.65 to 2.12; p=0.281). In contrast, ideal multinomial sampling predicts a reduction from 2.68% to 0.75%. Higher shot counts reduce block-to-block variability but do not recover the mean cold-side temperature field. The results show that full-field readout of a strongly nonuniform thermal state is limited by more than ordinary finite-shot sampling under the tested hardware conditions, motivating observable-focused quantum formulations for heat flux, stored energy, and selected temperatures.
