## 摘要
我们提出了一个端到端框架，用于利用量子模拟估计粒子数守恒二次量子化哈密顿量的正则热力学性质。该框架将混合 Dicke 态的初态制备、粒子数守恒的 Trotter 化模拟以及广义量子信号处理相结合，以估计正则配分函数和可观测量。在此正则方案基础上，我们引入一种巨正则重构方案，其中通过经典重加权，同一量子测量数据可复用于任意化学势，从而避免在改变化学势时重复执行量子电路。我们证明存在与系统尺寸无关的温度区间，在其中对于物理相关的哈密顿量，正则方案和重构的巨正则方案在相差多项式因子的意义下，都具有比经典 Lanczos 方法更低的渐近时间复杂度。我们针对弱耦合和中等耦合情形下的二维 Hubbard 模型，对理论结果进行了数值验证。

## Abstract
We develop an end-to-end framework for estimating canonical thermal properties of particle-number-conserving second-quantized Hamiltonians using quantum simulations. The framework integrates initial state preparation with mixed Dicke states, particle-number-preserving Trotterized simulation, and generalized quantum signal processing to estimate canonical partition functions and observables. Building on this canonical scheme, we introduce a grand-canonical reconstruction scheme in which the same quantum measurement data can be reused for arbitrary chemical potentials through classical reweighting, thereby avoiding repeated quantum circuit executions as the chemical potential is varied. We prove the existence of system-size-independent temperature regimes in which both the canonical and reconstructed grand-canonical schemes have lower asymptotic time complexity than classical Lanczos methods, up to polynomial factors, for physically relevant Hamiltonians. We numerically validate our theoretical results for the two-dimensional Hubbard model in both weak- and intermediate-coupling regimes.
