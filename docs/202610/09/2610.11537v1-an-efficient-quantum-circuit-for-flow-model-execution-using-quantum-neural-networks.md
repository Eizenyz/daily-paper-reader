## 摘要
流模型通过求解由速度场定义的常微分方程，生成从初始分布到目标分布的轨迹。流匹配通过对两个分布之间的输运动力学建模来学习该速度场。波函数流通过引入连续性哈密顿量，在流模型与量子动力学之间建立形式化联系，该哈密顿量驱动量子态的薛定谔演化。本文研究波函数流的精确且高效量子模拟，从而在量子计算机上实现流模型的高效执行。我们首先利用基于量子只读存储器（QROM）的相位回踢框架进行波函数流模拟，生成与相应传统流模型所产生概率密度高度匹配的概率密度。为解决高电路资源开销问题，我们进一步将训练好的量子神经网络（QNN）融入相位回踢框架，以替代QROM进行数据编码。数值实验表明，我们提出的方法在量子计算机上更高效地实现流模型，因为与基于QROM的框架相比，它保持了波函数流模拟的精度，并显著减少了电路资源。

## Abstract
Flow models generate trajectories from an initial distribution to a target distribution by solving an ordinary differential equation defined by a velocity field. Flow matching learns this velocity field by modeling the transport dynamics between the two distributions. Wavefunction flow establishes a formal connection between flow models and quantum dynamics by introducing a continuity Hamiltonian, which drives the Schrödinger evolution of quantum states. In this paper, we investigate accurate and efficient quantum simulation of the wavefunction flow, thereby realizing the efficient implementation of flow models on quantum computers. We first leverage a quantum read-only memory (QROM)-based phase kickback framework for the wavefunction flow simulation, generating probability densities that closely match those produced by the corresponding conventional flow model. To address the high circuit-resource cost, we further incorporate a trained quantum neural network (QNN) into the phase kickback framework, replacing QROM for data encoding. Numerical experiments demonstrate that our proposed method implements flow models on quantum computers more efficiently, since it maintains the accuracy of wavefunction flow simulation compared with the QROM-based framework, and significantly reduces the circuit resources.
