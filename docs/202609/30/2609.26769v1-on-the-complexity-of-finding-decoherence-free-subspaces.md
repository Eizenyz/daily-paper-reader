## 摘要
无退相干子空间是开放量子系统的一种稳态结构，它保持位于其中的态之间的量子相干性，因此在量子信息科学与技术中获得了多种应用。本文研究判定一个开放量子系统是否容许无退相干子空间的计算复杂度。更具体地说，我们在马尔可夫开放量子系统的背景下研究该问题，这类系统由不含时的 Lindblad 主方程描述。在此过程中，我们引入 $k$-局域 Lindbladian 问题，它刻画了在 Lindbladian 动力学下计算纯度衰减率的难度。我们证明，当局域性 $k \geq 5$ 时，这两个问题对复杂度类 Quantum Merlin Arthur (QMA) 都是困难的，其中第一个问题在完美完备性下是 QMA-困难的，第二个问题则是 QMA-完全的。我们的困难性构造通过将量子电路的执行编码到一个 Lindbladian 的稳态子空间中，将 Kitaev 的时钟哈密顿量构造推广到开放量子系统情形，该子空间包含纯历史态和混合历史态。该子空间随后会根据所编码电路的输出而呈现混合性。我们的结果表明，判定一个一般的马尔可夫开放量子系统是否容许无退相干子空间，即使对于量子计算而言也是难解的。

## Abstract
Decoherence free subspaces are a steady-state structure of the open quantum system which preserves quantum coherence between the states lying with in it and thus has found a variety of applications throughout quantum information science and technology. In this paper we study the computational complexity of deciding whether an open quantum system admits a decoherence free subspace or not. More specifically we study this problem with in the context of Markovian open quantum systems, governed by the time-independent Lindblad master equation. Along the way we introduce the $k$-Local Lindbladian problem, which captures the difficulty of computing purity decay rates under Lindbladian dynamics. We show that both problems are hard for the complexity class Quantum Merlin Arthur (QMA) when the locality $k \geq 5$, with the first under perfect completeness and the second being complete for QMA. Our hardness construction generalizes Kitaev's clock Hamiltonian construction to the open quantum system setting by encoding the execution of a quantum circuit into the steady subspace of a Lindbladian containing both pure and mixed history states. This subspace is then mixed depending on the output of the encoded circuit. Our results suggest that deciding whether a generic Markovian open quantum system admits a decoherence free subspace is intractable even for quantum computation.
