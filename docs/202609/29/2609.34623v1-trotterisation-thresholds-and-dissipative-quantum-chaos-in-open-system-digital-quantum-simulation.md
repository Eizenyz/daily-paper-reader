## 摘要
数字量子模拟（DQS）是量子计算最有前景的应用之一。Trotter化DQS通过将演化分解为一系列基于门的Trotter步骤来模拟量子动力学。使用更大的Trotter步长可以减少所需的门数量，但现在已经认识到，Trotter化步长不能超过某个阈值，超过该阈值后，由于量子混沌的出现，模拟精度会突然崩溃。迄今为止，这种行为仅在不受环境噪声和退相干影响的封闭系统中得到研究，而现实量子计算机对这些效应高度敏感。在这项工作中，我们研究了一系列与实验相关的开放系统量子模拟器模型，并表明即使包含开放系统效应，甚至在所有相干性都已丧失的长时间稳态极限下，Trotter化阈值仍然存在。我们表明，开放DQS能够在阈值之前准确再现开放目标系统的动力学，但在超过阈值后仍会表现出性能的突然崩溃。我们引入了新的统计技术，基于随机矩阵理论（RMT），并在为封闭系统开发的技术基础上，严格刻画超过Trotter化阈值后量子混沌的出现，既包括近似幺正的量子混沌，也包括真正的耗散量子混沌。最后，我们证明，当耗散与底层幺正量子混沌的存在达到平衡时，耗散量子混沌会出现；耗散必须足够强以诱导真正的耗散行为，但又不能强到抑制底层的混沌结构。

## Abstract
Digital quantum simulation (DQS) is one of the most promising applications of quantum computing. Trotterised DQS models quantum dynamics by decomposing the evolution into a sequence of gate-based Trotter steps. Using larger Trotter steps reduces the required gate count, but it is now understood that the Trotterisation step size cannot be increased beyond a certain threshold value, beyond which there is a sudden breakdown in simulation accuracy, due to the onset of quantum chaos. To date, this behaviour has only been studied for closed systems not subjected to the environmental noise and decoherence to which real-world quantum computers are highly sensitive. In this work, we study a range of experimentally relevant open-system quantum simulator models and show that Trotterisation thresholds persist even when open-system effects are included, even in the long-time, steady-state limit, once all coherence has been lost. We show that open DQS can accurately reproduce the dynamics of open target systems prior to the threshold, but still exhibits a sudden performance breakdown beyond it. We introduce new, statistical techniques to rigorously characterise the onset of quantum chaos beyond the Trotterisation threshold for both approximately unitary and genuine dissipative quantum chaos, based on random matrix theory (RMT), building on techniques developed for closed systems. Finally, we demonstrate that dissipative quantum chaos emerges when the dissipation is in balance with the presence of underlying unitary quantum chaos; the dissipation must be strong enough to induce genuinely dissipative behaviour, yet not so strong as to suppress the underlying chaotic structure.
